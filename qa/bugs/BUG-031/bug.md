# BUG-031 · Table cell text artefacts: leader dots, line-break hyphens, a lost plus-minus sign or a repeated legend letter; values and columns are kept

**Suspected module:** docling table cell text (unconfirmed)

## Description

Table cells keep small text artefacts. Values, order and columns are right, so the defect is Low. Seen patterns: leader dots in Contents tables ('Overview . . . . . 1-1', and the page cell starts with '. '), line-break hyphens kept with a space ('emis- sion'), a plus-minus sign read as '+', and a legend letter written twice ('a a module name'). Seen in LOGIQ_S8 (6 Contents tables), Philips (4) and 2002 (1) in the final versions.

## Root cause

Not confirmed. Probably the table cell text is taken as printed, with no clean-up of leader dots, hyphens at line breaks or special glyphs.

## What was done

- 2026-10-07: created from the TRIAGE rows of the final re-measurement, by request of the reviewer (Oscar Munoz). Proposal: ../doc/propuestas/PROPUESTA_REGLAS_TRIAGE_2026-10-07.md.

## Fix

Proposed (deterministic, no AI): clean cell text after the table is read. Remove runs of leader dots, join words split by a line-break hyphen, and keep the plus-minus glyph. Test with the LOGIQ_S8 Contents tables and the Philips tables on p457–458.

Not fixed yet.
